Business Challenges

A complex, inherited setup across three decentralized markets

When Quentin joined Eaglestone as Group Consolidation Manager in July 2025, he inherited a Prophix environment configured by a predecessor without formal consolidation training. The structure was built bottom-up rather than top-down, sub-consolidations were not linked to the group consolidation, and France, a key activity cluster, ran its own consolidation in SAP BFC, sending only pre-aggregated contributions to the group level.

This created real visibility problems. Quentin had no access to the underlying intercompany eliminations, local data, or console adjustments for France. When questions arose from management or from IFRS-reporting shareholders, he had to go back to France for every detail. Deadlines were regularly missed. The group operates across Belgium, France, and Luxembourg with differing accounting systems and a dual reporting requirement: local Luxembourgian standards alongside IFRS.

More transparency, faster information
The information is much more transparent, especially since France is now directly integrated into the system. I'm much faster at getting the information out.
Standout Features

What Quentin and his team rely on most

Write your cAsked what he and his team use most, Quentin pointed to a handful of capabilities he now relies on daily rather than a single headline feature.

  • Intercompany reconciliation module: his preferred way to reconcile positions between entities, which he described as far more user friendly than the tool he used previously.
  • Custom reporting layer (DynProfit): lets him build detailed reports that go well beyond the standard consolidated contributions his predecessor relied on.
  • Analytical dimensions: used to answer specific management requests, such as pulling bank debt by banking establishment directly out of the consolidation.
  • Coherence controls in the liasses: validation rules, including some Quentin added himself, that catch errors such as assets not matching liabilities before consolidation runs.
  • Segment-level consolidation: the ability to release a consolidated view by business segment, not just by country, something the previous setup could not produce.
  • Scenario simulation: modeling the consolidated impact of a deal structure or an investor's dilution before it happens, to compare options before committing.

Learning by doing with the CSP
The work we do with these consultants: we train ourselves by working. They guide and supervise us, and we hold the keys. It is the best way to understand the tool.
