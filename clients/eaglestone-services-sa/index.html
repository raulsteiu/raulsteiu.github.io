How Eaglestone Services SA transformed financial consolidation with Prophix

Listen to Quentin, Group Consolidation Manager at Eaglestone, share how Prophix Financial Consolidation and the Continuous Success Package helped him restructure a complex inherited setup, integrate France into the group consolidation, and build real-time visibility across three markets

Business Challenges

A complex, inherited setup across three decentralized markets

When Quentin joined Eaglestone as Group Consolidation Manager in July 2025, he inherited a Prophix environment configured by a predecessor without formal consolidation training. The structure was built bottom-up rather than top-down, sub-consolidations were not linked to the group consolidation, and France ran its own consolidation in SAP BFC, sending only pre-aggregated contributions to the group level. This created real visibility problems. Quentin had no access to the underlying intercompany eliminations, local data, or console adjustments for France. When questions arose from management or from IFRS-reporting shareholders, he had to go back to France for every detail. Deadlines were regularly missed. The group operates across Belgium, France, and Luxembourg with differing accounting systems and a dual reporting requirement — local Luxembourgian standards alongside IFRS.

Key Results

From inherited complexity to full consolidation independence

With the restructured Prophix setup, Eaglestone now runs a top-down group consolidation with sub-consolidations for Belgium, France, and Luxembourg linked in real time. Quentin has full visibility into intercompany eliminations, local data, and console adjustments for every entity — detail that was simply unavailable before. The quality and traceability of consolidated figures improved significantly. Quentin builds detailed reporting using Prophix's DynProfit layer, analyses segment-level contributions, and reconciles consolidated accounts against treasury and business plan data — work that previously had no time allocated to it. The closing cycle shortened by approximately one week per period. France was fully migrated from SAP BFC into Prophix during 2024. Investor-facing IFRS reporting now meets strict deadlines consistently. The team moved from spending most of its energy keeping consolidation running to spending that energy on analysis.

Clip 1 — More transparency, faster information (09:02)
"The information is much more transparent — especially since France is now directly integrated into the system. I'm much faster at getting the information out."

Who is Eaglestone Services SA?

Eaglestone is a real estate promotion and development group headquartered in Luxembourg, operating across Belgium, France, and Luxembourg. The group reports under both local Luxembourgian statutory standards and IFRS, serving investors including at least one listed company shareholder.
